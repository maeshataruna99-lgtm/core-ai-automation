# Shipping

This document is synthetic sample data for the demo company, Northwind Parts.

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within Java. Shipping to other islands takes 7 to 14 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in Jakarta, Bandung, and Surabaya.

## Costs

Standard shipping is free for orders above IDR 500,000. Express shipping always costs IDR 75,000.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email when an order is dispatched. Orders placed after 15:00 are dispatched on the next business day.
