# Refund policy

This document is synthetic sample data for the demo company, Northwind Parts.

## Time limit

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days, staff can offer store credit only, and a team lead must approve it.

## Condition of the item

Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Damaged items are refunded in full, including the shipping cost, when the customer reports the damage within 7 days of delivery.

## Payment

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Bank transfers take up to 5 business days. Card refunds take up to 10 business days.
