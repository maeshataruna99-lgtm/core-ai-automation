# Bulk orders

This document is synthetic sample data for the demo company, Northwind Parts.

## Discounts

Orders above 100 units get a 10% discount. Orders above 500 units get a 15% discount.

## Quotes

A written quote is valid for 14 days. Bulk orders have a lead time of 10 business days after the quote is accepted.

## Payment

Approved business accounts can pay by invoice on net 30 terms. All other customers pay in full before dispatch.
