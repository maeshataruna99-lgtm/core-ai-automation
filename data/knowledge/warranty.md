# Warranty

This document is synthetic sample data for the demo company, Northwind Parts.

## Coverage

All parts have a standard warranty of 12 months from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover normal wear or misuse.

## Claims

To make a claim, send the order number and a photo of the defect. The quality team answers within 5 business days. Approved claims get a replacement part, shipped free of charge.
